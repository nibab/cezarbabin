About Essays Projects

Hi, I'm Cezar.

I write here about software, AI, and the economics of distribution. I spent time building consumer and infrastructure products at Amazon, and have been around the AI-application space long enough to be skeptical of the framing the industry currently sells.

Most of what I publish on this site is an attempt to clarify my own thinking on questions I keep returning to — how vertical software gets sold, why open source might finally be ready to eat business apps, and what kinds of distribution architectures hold up when the cost of building software collapses toward zero.

If something here sparks a thought, the fastest way to reach me is @n1babc on X.
